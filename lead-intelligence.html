Collector Automation

Whizz now has the ingestion, scoring, deduplication and callback layer. The browser collector runs separately through LEAD_COLLECTOR_URL. When a source requests CAPTCHA, 2FA or device verification, the collector should return verification_required with a verification URL. Whizz displays that checkpoint instead of attempting to bypass the protection. After verification, the collector resumes and posts new records to the callback.

Required Worker secrets/config: LEAD_INTELLIGENCE_KEY for credential encryption; LEAD_COLLECTOR_URL and optional LEAD_COLLECTOR_TOKEN for the Playwright/browser collector.
